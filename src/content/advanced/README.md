# Advanced Management and Strategy modules

One YAML file per module, named by its id in `MODULE_PLAN` (src/lib/advanced.ts) and checked against `advancedModuleSchema` (src/lib/advanced-schema.ts) and `checkAdvanced` (src/lib/integrity-advanced.ts). Pages: `/advanced/`, `/advanced/<domain>/` and `/advanced/<domain>/<id>/`.

Every module has eight parts in order: conceptual foundation, advanced explanation, models and frameworks, real business applications, case studies, critical evaluation, a decision exercise and further reading. It is beyond the IB course and never counts towards it. Real organizations appear only with dated facts from sources in the register (`src/content/sources/`); illustrative examples and fictional cases are labelled.
