# Design system

The Business Brief should feel like a well-run boardroom paper: an ink cover, paper pages, ruled sections, numbered exhibits, a decision waiting to be made. It shares the Economics site's standards (accuracy, accessibility, restraint) and none of its visual identity.

## Identity

### The idea

A *brief* is the document a decision-maker reads before deciding. The mark is a brief with a folded brass corner; inside it, a path arrives at a decision node and splits: the path taken continues, the path not taken stays grey. It says what the platform does: situation, decision, consequence.

| | Arjun Agrawal Economics | The Business Brief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metaphor | Models and diagrams | Briefs, case files, board papers, decisions |
| Surface | The Economics site's own palette | Ink, paper, slate; cobalt for the learner's choice, brass for the document |
| Shapes | The Economics site's own forms | Square corners (0 to 4 px), rules, numbered tabs, a hard offset shadow for objects that can be picked up |
| Signature object | Interactive diagrams | The decision memo; the board pack that changes as you decide |

### Marks

All masters are outlined SVG in `public/brand/`, generated by `scripts/brand/build_brand.py` (outlines from the font files with fontTools and HarfBuzz, so they render without the fonts).

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |

| `business-brief-mark-reversed.svg` | The mark on ink |
| `business-brief-mark-mono.svg` | One colour, for single-colour printing |
| `business-brief-lockup.svg`, `-reversed.svg` | Mark with "Arjun Agrawal" and "The Business Brief": the default logo |
| `business-brief-lockup-stacked.svg`, `-reversed.svg` | Stacked version for square spaces |
| `business-brief-wordmark.svg`, `-reversed.svg` | The name alone |

Rules:

- Clear space around the mark: at least a quarter of its width on every side.
- Minimum size: 16 px for the mark, 120 px wide for the lockup.
- Do not recolour, outline, rotate, add effects to, or place the mark on a busy image. On ink, use the reversed files.
- The **Arjun Agrawal monogram** (`public/brand/creator/`, the creator's authentic asset, unchanged) is a creator signature, used in the footer and on the About page. It is never the platform's logo, and the platform's mark is never placed inside it.

## Type

| Family | Role | Token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inter Tight | Display: headings, the decision question, the answer in a working | `--font-display` |
| Inter | Body text and interface | `--font-body` |
| IBM Plex Mono | Labels, tags, codes, numbers, working | `--font-mono` |
| Source Serif 4 | Case narrative, the learner's role, consequences, quotations | `--font-serif` |

All four are under the SIL Open Font Licence and self-hosted from `public/fonts/`, with their licence files beside them. Labels are mono, uppercase, letter-spaced. Numbers in tables and working use tabular figures. The scale runs from `--fs-label` (0.75 rem) to `--fs-display` (fluid, up to 5.75 rem); headings tighten their tracking as they grow.

The serif face is the case's voice: anything a person in a case says, or anything that happens to them, is set in it. Analysis and instruction are set in Inter.

## Space, layout and shape

- 4 px base; tokens `--sp-1` to `--sp-9`.
- Page width 80 rem; reading column 42 rem; fluid gutter from 16 px.
- Corners 0 to 4 px. Documents have corners; nothing is a pill or a rounded card.
- Rules do the structural work: a heavy ink rule opens a section, hairlines separate rows.
- A hard offset shadow (no blur) marks an object you can pick up: the decision memo, a chosen Decision Room paper, the creator portrait.
- No gradients, glows or glass. The only pattern is the hatch for displaced sales in the special order bar, where it encodes data.

## Charts

Charts are SVG drawn by `src/lib/charts.ts`:

- Total revenue is cobalt, total cost ink, fixed costs dashed slate; profit and loss regions are tinted and labelled in words.
- Every chart has a `<title>` and a `<desc>` that states its result, and its figures in a table beside it.
- Labels carry a paper-coloured halo so they stay legible where they cross a line.
- A truncated axis is stated in the description and the caption.
- On narrow screens a chart keeps a minimum width of 32 rem and scrolls inside its own focusable region, so its text never shrinks below legibility.

## Motion

Short (120 to 200 ms) and functional: disclosure arrows and bar widths. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Print

`src/styles/print.css` removes the site header and footer and the controls that only work on screen, keeps exhibits, tables and workings unbroken, and adds a line of attribution with the page address. `src/scripts/print.ts` opens every disclosure before printing (answers, mark schemes, workings) and closes them again afterwards. Revision briefs are designed to fit two sides of A4.
